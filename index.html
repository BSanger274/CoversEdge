How to run CoversEdge

Every day · about 2 minutes

  1. Open this page. Your Odds API key is saved, and the last board you pulled shows instantly — no API call, no credits spent.
  2. To pull today's fresh lines, click ⬇ Fetch Lines once. (This is the only thing that spends credits.)
  3. To compare against your Covers pool, expand ⚡ Pool Lines and follow its 3 steps: open your pool pick page → paste the one-line console command it gives you → click ⬇ Load from Clipboard, then Fetch Lines.
Protecting your monthly credits: one Fetch Lines ≈ 12 of your 500 monthly credits, so ~40 pulls a month. The ⚡ credits-left counter under the key box shows how many remain. Leave ↻ Auto-Refresh on OFF (the default) and just click Fetch when you want an update — auto-refresh every 60s would drain the month in a single evening.

First-time setup · once

  1. Get a free key at the-odds-api.com — no credit card needed.
  2. Paste it into the The-Odds-API Key box and click ⬇ Fetch Lines.
  3. It's remembered from then on — you won't need to paste it again on this device.

No key handy?

  • Click Use Demo Data for a full sample board with today's date, so you can see the layout without a key.
